# Home page and blog (whole site)

Scope: index.html, blog.html, blog/*.html. Visitor mode: Experience on the home page (the protein work leads), Read on blog posts.
Audience and job: PIs, admissions and biotech/AI people skim research and reach out; everyone reads the blog. Balanced success. Content, section order, blog features and the protein-name opening are fixed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a structure atlas: proteins charted the way a star atlas charts the sky, with the real PDB entries behind the name as the plotted objects. It refuses both the neon dark developer portfolio and the plain academic page.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy chart ground with a faint coordinate grid; chalk-white type and points; amber for annotations and links; one red-orange reserved for the object under the eye (hover ring, primary action). Names in light italic book serif, coordinates and labels in a small tracked sans with tabular figures. Hairline frames with node points at their ends; dot diameter carries hierarchy.

STORY: The visitor sees noise resolve into four real protein structures that spell a name, learns each is a catalogued PDB entry, then reads research and writing laid out as charted objects.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On first visit, a field of points denoises into the atoms of the wordmark, and the cartoon render fades in over them. The wordmark spans most of the width as a chart plate, PDB codes labelled in amber above each letter; the name sits large in italic beneath at left; the index of sections with magnitude dots sits top right. No button row.

FORM: Celestial star atlas chart, a dealt challenger chosen by the user over the assigned direction; seed:d9dc30a2be14a0cf. Signature interaction: pointing at a letter rings it in red and names the protein behind it. Motion grammar: one emergence (the denoising intro), then stillness; the wordmark docks into the header on scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
